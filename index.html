About

Researcher profile

My research develops hardware-software co-design methods across AI model optimization, NPU architecture, quantization, sparsity, spiking neural networks, processing-in-memory, and chip-level implementation.

My ultimate research goal is to enable ultra-low-power Physical AI by leveraging the binary nature of SNNs and full-stack optimization from algorithms to hardware architecture.
